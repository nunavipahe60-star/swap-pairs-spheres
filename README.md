# Swap Pairs on Spheres of Revolution: What the Equivariant Spectrum Sees

**Author:** Erina (`nunavipahe60-star`), with collaborative research assistance from Claude, Gemini, and GPT/Codex.

## Overview

Fix three nonzero nonnegative bumps with separated supports on the northern half of a round sphere $S^2$. Each bump can be placed at its given latitude or at its antipodal/equatorial reflection. Although all coefficients in the local semiclassical expansion agree across this entire family of rotation-invariant metrics, the equivariant Laplace spectrum distinguishes these arrangements up to a simultaneous reflection.

Key highlights of the 15-page manuscript:
- **Exact inverse traces & Two-Ruler mechanism:** The distinction comes from exact inverse traces and from the mismatch between meridian arc length ($m = 0$ sector) and the Mercator isothermal coordinate ($m \neq 0$ sector).
- **Finite-trace indistinguishability via Borsuk–Ulam:** For any prescribed finite number of initial inverse traces, a family with enough bumps admits distinct non-isometric arrangements agreeing on all prescribed traces.
- **Unconditional three-bump sign uniqueness:** Combining the $m=0$ and $m\neq 0$ sectors settles sign uniqueness for three-bump swap pairs without semiclassical ambiguity.
